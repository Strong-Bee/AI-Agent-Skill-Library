    - "Performance Measurement: KPIs, attribution, ROI, iterative improvement"

    - "Integrate SEO best practices into content planning and creation"
    - "Establish KPIs and measurement frameworks"
    - "Analyze performance data and iterate strategy"
    - "Collaborate with SEO, design, product, sales, and leadership"

  industry_terminology:
    - term: "Content Pillar"
      definition: "Core topic or theme that anchors a content cluster with comprehensive coverage"
      context: "Typically 2,000+ words; links to 10-20+ cluster articles"
    - term: "Content Cluster"
      definition: "Group of interlinked content pieces around a central pillar topic"
      context: "Supports topical authority; improves SEO through internal linking"
    - term: "Editorial Calendar"
      definition: "Schedule of planned content with topics, formats, owners, deadlines, channels"
      context: "Living document; balances evergreen, timely, and campaign content"
    - term: "Content Brief"
      definition: "Document specifying requirements for a content piece (topic, angle, SEO, structure, CTA)"
      context: "Aligns writers/editors; reduces revisions; ensures SEO/brand compliance"
    - term: "Content Atomization"
      definition: "Breaking long-form content into smaller pieces for multi-channel distribution"
      context: "E.g., webinar → blog post → social clips → email series → infographic"

  professional_methodologies:
    - name: "Content Audit Framework"
      description: "Systematic approach to evaluating content portfolio"
      steps:
        - "Inventory: Crawl all URLs; export from CMS, social, email, video platforms"
        - "Categorize: By topic, format, funnel stage, audience, owner, date"
        - "Quality Score: Rate each piece (accuracy, depth, uniqueness, E-E-A-T)"
        - "Performance Score: Traffic, engagement, conversions, SEO rankings"
        - "Gap Analysis: Missing topics, formats, funnel stages, audiences"
        - "Action Matrix: Create / Update / Consolidate / Redirect / Remove"
      when_to_use: "Annual audits, site migrations, strategy resets, new client onboarding"
      limitations: "Time-intensive; requires tool access; subjective quality scoring"

    - name: "Topic Cluster Development Process"
      description: "Systematic approach to building topical authority"
      steps:
        - "Identify core topics aligned with business and search demand"
        - "Research keyword clusters (Ahrefs/SEMrush, GSC, PAA, forums)"
        - "Map keywords to pillar page + 10-20 cluster articles"
        - "Prioritize by search volume, difficulty, business value, intent"
        - "Create pillar page (comprehensive, 3000+ words, TOC, multimedia)"
        - "Produce cluster articles (link to/from pillar, consistent structure)"
        - "Implement internal linking hub-and-spoke architecture"
      when_to_use: "New site launch, content strategy reset, SEO growth initiative"
      limitations: "Resource-intensive; long time to ROI; requires sustained commitment"

    - name: "Content Production Workflow"
      description: "Standardized process from ideation to publication"
      steps:
        - "Ideation: Keyword research, competitor gaps, sales questions, trends"
        - "Briefing: Content brief with SEO, angle, structure, examples, CTA"
        - "Drafting: Writer creates first draft per brief and style guide"
        - "Editing: Substantive edit → line edit → fact-check → SEO review"
        - "Production: Design assets, video, interactive elements"
        - "QA: Accessibility, mobile, schema, links, tracking"
        - "Publish: CMS, scheduling, metadata, social cards"
        - "Promote: Email, social, paid, outreach, repurposing"
        - "Measure: 30/60/90 day check-ins; refresh queue"
      when_to_use: "All content production; scale with team size"
      limitations: "Requires defined roles; bottlenecks at editing/QA"

  best_practices:
    - practice: "Quality Over Quantity"
      description: "Publish fewer, better pieces; depth beats frequency for SEO and authority"
      rationale: "Google rewards comprehensive, original content; thin content penalized"
      anti_pattern: "Daily blog posts of 500 words with no original insight"
    - practice: "Write for Humans, Optimize for Search"
      description: "Satisfy user intent first; then layer on SEO requirements"
      rationale: "Google's helpful content system rewards user-centric content"
      anti_pattern: "Keyword stuffing; writing for algorithms not people"
    - practice: "Build Content Moats"
      description: "Create content competitors can't easily replicate (original research, data, expertise)"
      rationale: "Defensible competitive advantage; attracts links and authority"
      anti_pattern: "Commodity content easily replicated by AI or competitors"
    - practice: "Repurpose Systematically"
      description: "Atomize every major piece into 10+ derivative assets"
      rationale: "Maximizes ROI; reaches different audiences; reinforces message"
      anti_pattern: "Publish once and forget; single-channel distribution"
    - practice: "Measure What Matters"
      description: "Track leading indicators (rankings, engagement) and lagging (revenue, pipeline)"
      rationale: "Vanity metrics don't justify budget; connect to business outcomes"
      anti_pattern: "Reporting only pageviews or social likes without conversion context"

  common_mistakes:
    - mistake: "No Defined Strategy"
      consequence: "Random acts of content; wasted resources; no measurable impact"
      prevention: "Document strategy with goals, audiences, pillars, KPIs before creating"
    - mistake: "Ignoring Distribution"
      consequence: "Great content that nobody sees; wasted production investment"
      prevention: "Plan distribution before creation; build amplification into workflow"
    - mistake: "Keyword-First, User-Last"
      consequence: "Content ranks but doesn't convert; high bounce, low engagement"
      prevention: "Start with user questions and intent; layer keywords naturally"
    - mistake: "Set-and-Forget Content"
      consequence: "Content decays; rankings drop; information becomes outdated"
      prevention: "Quarterly content audits; refresh program; decay alerts in GSC"
    - mistake: "Siloed Content Teams"
      consequence: "Inconsistent messaging; duplicated effort; missed SEO opportunities"
      prevention: "Cross-functional content council; shared calendar; unified strategy"

workflow:
  step_1_request_analysis:
    description: "Analyze business goals, target audiences, current content landscape, resources, constraints"
    inputs:
      - "Business objectives (revenue, leads, brand awareness, retention)"
      - "Target audiences (personas, JTBD, content preferences)"
      - "Current content inventory and performance"
      - "Competitive content landscape"
      - "Resources (team, budget, tools, timeline)"
    outputs:
      - "Content opportunity assessment"
      - "Strategic priorities and quick wins"
      - "Resource requirement estimates"
    validation: "Clear business alignment; realistic scope; stakeholder buy-in"

  step_2_requirement_clarification:
    description: "Clarify content goals, audience priorities, format preferences, distribution channels, KPIs"
    inputs:
      - "Opportunity assessment"
    outputs:
      - "Prioritized content goals (traffic, leads, revenue, retention)"
      - "Primary/secondary audiences with content preferences"
      - "Content formats and channels by audience segment"
      - "Success metrics and measurement plan"
      - "Brand voice, style guide, compliance requirements"
    validation: "Goals are SMART; audiences defined; metrics agreed"

  step_3_context_gathering:
    description: "Gather data: content audit, keyword research, competitor analysis, audience research, sales insights"
    inputs:
      - "Clarified requirements"
    outputs:
      - "Content audit report (inventory, quality, performance, gaps)"
      - "Keyword research with clusters, intent, difficulty, volume"
      - "Competitor content analysis (topics, formats, gaps, distribution)"
      - "Audience research (interviews, surveys, sales calls, analytics)"
      - "Sales/customer success feedback on content needs"
    validation: "Comprehensive data foundation; gaps identified"

  step_4_research:
    description: "Research content trends, format innovations, distribution channels, SEO opportunities"
    inputs:
      - "Context data"
    outputs:
      - "Topic cluster opportunities with keyword data"
      - "Content format trends by channel and audience"
      - "Distribution channel analysis (owned, earned, paid)"
      - "SEO opportunity sizing (traffic potential, difficulty)"
      - "Competitive content gaps and differentiation angles"
    validation: "Data-driven opportunities; validated with stakeholder input"

  step_5_planning:
    description: "Create content strategy document, editorial calendar, resource plan, production workflow"
    inputs:
      - "Research findings"
      - "Business requirements"
    outputs:
      - "Content strategy document (goals, audiences, pillars, KPIs)"
      - "Topic cluster map with pillar/cluster assignments"
      - "Quarterly editorial calendar with themes and cadence"
      - "Resource plan (internal, freelance, agency, tools budget)"
      - "Production workflow and approval process"
      - "Distribution and amplification plan"
    validation: "Plan traceable to goals; realistic resourcing; dependencies mapped"

  step_6_task_execution:
    description: "Execute content production: briefs, drafting, editing, design, publishing, distribution"
    inputs:
      - "Editorial calendar and content briefs"
    outputs:
      - "Published content (blog, video, podcast, social, email, gated)"
      - "SEO-optimized with schema, internal links, metadata"
      - "Distributed across owned, earned, paid channels"
      - "Email/social promotion executed per plan"
      - "Tracking implemented (UTMs, events, conversions)"
    validation: "Content meets brief; SEO checks pass; published on schedule"

  step_7_validation:
    description: "Measure early performance: traffic, engagement, rankings, conversions, feedback"
    inputs:
      - "Published content"
      - "Baseline KPIs"
    outputs:
      - "30/60/90 day performance dashboards"
      - "Top/bottom performer analysis"
      - "Engagement quality assessment (scroll, time, video completion)"
      - "Conversion attribution and assisted conversions"
      - "Audience feedback (comments, shares, survey)"
    validation: "Leading indicators trending positive; no major issues"

  step_8_quality_review:
    description: "Comprehensive content performance review against strategy and KPIs"
    inputs:
      - "Performance data (quarterly/bi-annually)"
    outputs:
      - "Content scorecard (traffic, engagement, conversions, ROI)"
      - "Top performer analysis (what worked, why, replicate)"
      - "Underperformer diagnosis (content, distribution, SEO, offer)"
      - "Content decay identification (ranking drops, traffic decline)"
      - "Strategy alignment check (goals, audiences, pillars)"
    validation: "Data-driven insights; clear optimization priorities"

  step_9_error_correction:
    description: "Optimize underperformers, refresh decaying content, fill gaps, iterate strategy"
    inputs:
      - "Quality review findings"
    outputs:
      - "Content refresh queue (priority, scope, deadline)"
      - "New content commissions for identified gaps"
      - "Distribution strategy adjustments"
      - "Strategy pivots based on learnings"
      - "Updated editorial calendar and resource plan"
    validation: "Improved metrics on refreshed content; gaps closing"

  step_10_final_delivery:
    description: "Deliver strategy report, updated roadmap, team handoff, stakeholder presentation"
    inputs:
      - "Validated strategy and optimized content"
    outputs:
      - "Annual/quarterly content strategy report"
      - "Updated content strategy document"
      - "Next quarter editorial calendar"
      - "Team handoff documentation (processes, tools, contacts)"
      - "Stakeholder presentation (results, insights, roadmap)"
    validation: "Stakeholder sign-off; team equipped; roadmap approved"

## Overview

The Content Strategist skill provides comprehensive capabilities for developing and executing content strategies that drive organic growth, engagement, and conversions across digital channels.

## Quick Start

```bash
# Essential content strategy toolkit
# 1. Set up GA4 + GSC for measurement
# 2. Configure Ahrefs/SEMrush for keyword research
# 3. Run content audit (Screaming Frog + analytics)
# 4. Conduct keyword research and topic clustering
# 5. Build editorial calendar in Notion/Airtable
# 6. Create content brief templates with SEO requirements
```

## Workflow Details

This skill applies the 10-step workflow for content strategy:

1. **Request Analysis** - Business goals, audiences, content landscape, resources
2. **Requirement Clarification** - Content goals, audiences, formats, channels, KPIs
3. **Context Gathering** - Content audit, keywords, competitors, audience research
4. **Research** - Topics, formats, distribution, SEO opportunities
5. **Planning** - Strategy doc, editorial calendar, resource plan, workflow
6. **Task Execution** - Briefs, drafting, editing, design, publishing, distribution
7. **Validation** - 30/60/90 day performance, engagement, rankings, conversions
8. **Quality Review** - Scorecard, top/bottom analysis, decay detection, alignment
9. **Error Correction** - Refresh queue, new commissions, distribution adjustments
10. **Final Delivery** - Strategy report, updated roadmap, handoff docs

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Content not ranking | Weak E-E-A-T, thin content, poor internal links | Deepen content; add expert quotes; build topic clusters |
| Low conversion | Intent mismatch, weak CTA, poor UX | Align with search intent; strengthen CTAs; improve page speed |
| Content decay | Algorithm updates, competitor content, outdated info | Quarterly audits; refresh program; monitoring alerts |
| Low engagement | Poor formatting, no multimedia, wall of text | Add headers, images, video, bullet points, interactive elements |
| Resource constraints | Small team, no budget, competing priorities | Prioritize high-ROI; repurpose; leverage UGC; outsource |
