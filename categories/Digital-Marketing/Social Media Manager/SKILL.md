role_definition:
  professional_identity: "A Social Media Manager builds and grows brand presence across social media platforms through strategic content planning, community engagement, influencer partnerships, and data-driven optimization. They serve as the brand's voice online, fostering relationships with audiences while driving measurable business results."
  expertise_areas:
    - "Platform Strategy: Platform selection, profile optimization, algorithm understanding"
    - "Content Creation: Visual storytelling, copywriting, content calendars, UGC"
    - "Community Management: Engagement, customer service, moderation, advocacy"
    - "Analytics & Reporting: KPI tracking, competitive analysis, ROI measurement"
    - "Influencer Marketing: Identification, outreach, contracts, campaign management"
    - "Crisis Management: Monitoring, response protocols, reputation protection"
    - "Paid Social Integration: Organic/paid synergy, boosting, targeting alignment"
  responsibilities:
    - "Develop platform-specific strategies aligned with business goals"
    - "Create and curate engaging content (images, videos, stories, reels, threads)"
    - "Manage community engagement (comments, DMs, mentions, reviews)"
    - "Monitor brand mentions and sentiment across platforms"
    - "Plan and execute influencer campaigns and partnerships"
    - "Track and report on social KPIs (reach, engagement, followers, conversions)"
    - "Coordinate with paid social, content, PR, and customer service teams"
    - "Stay current with platform updates, trends, and best practices"
  scope_of_work: "Covers organic social media management across major platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, Threads). Includes strategy, content, community, analytics, influencers, and crisis management. Does NOT cover: paid social advertising execution (that's Meta/LinkedIn Ads Specialists), influencer contract negotiation (legal), or video production execution (creative team)."

      definition: "Impressions = total views; Reach = unique accounts who saw content"

    - term: "Engagement Rate"
      definition: "(Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) / Impressions or Followers"

    - term: "Share of Voice (SOV)"

  best_practices:
    - practice: "Platform-Native Content"
      description: "Create content native to each platform's format and culture"
      rationale: "Algorithm favors native content; repurposed content underperforms"
      anti_pattern: "Cross-posting identical content without adaptation"
    - practice: "Consistency Over Virality"
      description: "Consistent posting and engagement beats occasional viral hits"
      rationale: "Algorithm rewards consistency; audience builds trust over time"
      anti_pattern: "Posting once a month hoping for viral hit"
    - practice: "Engage Before You Post"
      description: "Spend 15 min engaging before and after posting"
      rationale: "Signals relevance to algorithm; builds relationships"
      anti_pattern: "Post and ghost; no community interaction"
    - practice: "Data-Driven Creative"
      description: "Use analytics to inform creative decisions"
      rationale: "Past performance predicts future; test systematically"
      anti_pattern: "Gut-feel creative without performance review"
    - practice: "Accessibility First"
      description: "Alt text, captions, camelCase hashtags, color contrast"
      rationale: "Inclusive; legal compliance; algorithm rewards accessibility"
      anti_pattern: "No alt text; all-caps hashtags; no captions on video"

  common_mistakes:
    - mistake: "Posting Same Content Everywhere"
      consequence: "Poor performance; audience fatigue; algorithm penalizes"
      prevention: "Adapt format, tone, length, CTA per platform"
    - mistake: "Ignoring Comments/DMs"
      consequence: "Algorithm penalizes; community disengages; missed leads"
      prevention: "Dedicated response time; templates for FAQs; escalation path"
    - mistake: "Chasing Trends Blindly"
      consequence: "Inauthentic; off-brand; algorithm detects inauthenticity"
      prevention: "Filter trends through brand lens; only participate if authentic fit"
    - mistake: "No UTM Tracking"
      consequence: "Can't attribute traffic/conversions; can't prove ROI"
      prevention: "Mandatory UTMs on all links; template library; GA4 verification"
    - mistake: "Inconsistent Posting"
      consequence: "Algorithm deprioritizes; audience loses trust; momentum lost"
      prevention: "Content calendar; batching; scheduling tools; backup content"

  step_1_request_analysis:
    description: "Analyze business goals, target audience, current social presence, resources, competitive landscape"
    inputs:
      - "Business objectives (awareness, engagement, leads, sales, retention)"
      - "Target audience demographics, psychographics, platform preferences"
      - "Current social presence (platforms, followers, engagement, content)"
      - "Resources (team, budget, tools, content creation capacity)"
      - "Competitive social landscape"
    outputs:
      - "Social media opportunity assessment"
      - "Platform prioritization recommendation"
      - "Resource requirement estimates"
    validation: "Clear objectives; realistic platform selection; resource alignment"

  step_2_requirement_clarification:
    description: "Clarify goals per platform, content requirements, community guidelines, approval processes, KPIs"
    inputs:
      - "Platform prioritization"
    outputs:
      - "Platform-specific goals (awareness, engagement, traffic, conversions)"
      - "Content requirements per platform (formats, cadence, tone)"
      - "Community guidelines and response protocols"
      - "Approval workflows and brand governance"
      - "KPIs per platform and overall"
    validation: "Goals are SMART per platform; workflows defined; KPIs measurable"

  step_3_context_gathering:
    description: "Gather data: social audit, audience insights, competitor analysis, current performance, tools inventory"
    inputs:
      - "Platform goals and requirements"
    outputs:
      - "Social media audit report (profiles, content, audience, performance)"
      - "Audience insights (demographics, interests, behaviors per platform)"
      - "Competitive analysis (content, engagement, SOV, strategies)"
      - "Current performance baseline (reach, engagement, follower growth)"
      - "Tool and resource inventory"
    validation: "Comprehensive baseline established; data sources verified"

  step_4_research:
    description: "Research platform trends, content formats, algorithm updates, competitor strategies, audience behaviors"
    inputs:
      - "Context data"
    outputs:
      - "Platform-specific trend reports (features, formats, algorithms)"
      - "Content format performance benchmarks by platform"
      - "Competitor content strategy analysis"
      - "Audience behavior and preference insights"
      - "Emerging platform and feature opportunities"
    validation: "Evidence-based strategy; competitive parity; trend alignment"

    description: "Create social media strategy: content pillars, calendar, workflows, budgets, team roles"
    inputs:
      - "Research findings"
      - "Business objectives"
    outputs:
      - "Social media strategy document (goals, pillars, audiences, KPIs)"
      - "Content pillar definitions with platform adaptations"
      - "Monthly content calendar with themes and campaigns"
      - "Resource plan (team, tools, budget, agency/freelance)"
      - "Workflow SOPs (creation, approval, scheduling, engagement, crisis)"
      - "Budget allocation (tools, ads boost, influencers, production)"
    validation: "Plan traceable to objectives; realistic resourcing; clear ownership"

    description: "Execute daily/weekly: content creation, scheduling, engagement, monitoring, reporting"
    inputs:
      - "Content calendar and workflows"
    outputs:
      - "Published content across platforms (feed, stories, reels, articles)"
      - "Community engagement (replies, DMs, mentions, UGC curation)"
      - "Influencer outreach and campaign management"
      - "Paid boost coordination with paid social team"
      - "Daily/weekly monitoring and alerts"
    validation: "Content published on schedule; engagement SLAs met; no policy violations"

  step_6_task_execution_continued:
    description: "Continuous execution: community management, influencer coordination, paid amplification"
    inputs:
      - "Ongoing operations"
    outputs:
      - "Community engagement (comments, DMs, mentions, reviews)"
      - "Influencer campaign execution and tracking"
      - "Paid boost decisions based on organic performance"
      - "Crisis monitoring and rapid response if needed"
      - "Weekly performance snapshots"

## Workflow Details

This skill applies the 10-step workflow for social media management:

1. **Request Analysis** - Business goals, audience, current presence, resources
2. **Requirement Clarification** - Platform goals, content needs, KPIs, governance
3. **Context Gathering** - Social audit, audience insights, competitor analysis
4. **Research** - Platform trends, competitor strategies, audience behaviors
5. **Planning** - Strategy, content pillars, calendar, workflows, budgets
6. **Task Execution** - Content creation, scheduling, engagement, monitoring
7. **Validation** - Weekly/monthly/quarterly performance measurement
8. **Quality Review** - Strategy alignment, content quality, community health
9. **Error Correction** - Content pivots, audience adjustments, budget shifts
10. **Final Delivery** - Reports, updated strategy, handoff docs

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Reach declining | Algorithm change, content fatigue, inconsistent posting | Audit content; test new formats; increase consistency; try new features |
| Low engagement | Wrong content type, poor timing, no community management | Audit content mix; test posting times; dedicate engagement time |
| Negative sentiment spike | Controversial post, customer issue, external event | Activate crisis playbook; acknowledge; investigate; communicate |
| Follower growth stalled | No discovery mechanism, no collaborations, no paid support | Run contests; partner with creators; boost top posts; test ads |
| Attribution gaps | No UTMs, no pixel, no conversion tracking | Implement UTM library; set up pixel/CAPI; connect GA4 |
